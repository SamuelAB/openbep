Each month, every commitment in the BEP, the owner's included, gets a number. Firms that slip show up months before handover, and so does an owner who is slowing the work.

  • On target: Meets the target.
  • Slipping: Misses the target by 10% or less.
  • Off target: Misses the target by more than 10%. Three slipping months in a row also count as one month off target.
  • Steps: Each month off target in a row moves the party one step up the escalation ladder. See the steps
