Vague BIM is useless BIM.

OpenBEP builds a BIM Execution Plan from the uses the owner actually needs: who models what, to what level, by when, and how it is checked.

It is free and open. On this site your work stays in your own browser. For real projects, which hold private information, run your own copy.

BEP BuilderWrite your BIM Execution Plan step by step, or look at a finished example first (a school or a light rail line). Open a saved project fileContinue a plan you saved from the BEP Builder as a project file (.json). LOD specificationWhat each level of development means for each element type: the geometry and the parameters it must carry. Naming listsEvery OpenBEP name in one place: shared parameters, materials, line styles, text styles and families, with downloads, in English and in French. Owner trainingFor the owner's project manager and BIM representative: what to require, in 20 short sections, before the owner readiness test. Project scorecardTrack each party's BEP commitments month by month, the owner's included, and see who is slipping.
